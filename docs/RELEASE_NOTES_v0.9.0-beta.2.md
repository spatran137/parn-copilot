# PARN Copilot Agent v0.9.0-beta.2

First public beta of PARN, a Microsoft 365 Copilot agent that turns technician dictation and ticket evidence into drafts for ConnectWise.

## Added

- Copy-ready Problem, Action, Resolution, and Next Steps notes, plus separate suggestions for internal review.
- On-demand client email drafts that keep internal evidence private and distinguish solo work from shared work.
- Upload-ready ZIPs for internal knowledge review and vendor research in Copilot Agent Builder.
- A first-ticket walkthrough and fictional scenarios for testing attachments, VPN follow-ups, and email behavior.

## Improved

- Preserves performed steps, diagnostic results, and earlier context through ticket follow-ups.
- Distinguishes pending client confirmation, eligibility to close, and completed closure.
- Asks a clarifying question when ticket status or evidence conflicts.

## Known issues

- Copilot formatting and attachment interpretation can vary. Read drafts before pasting them into a ticket or email.
- Skill availability and results depend on tenant settings and permissions. Test both skills in your own tenant.
